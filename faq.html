About TCM

What is Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM)?

Traditional Chinese Medicine is a comprehensive, holistic medical system that focuses on restoring balance and promoting the natural healing abilities of the body. Shallowford Herbs practitioners use TCM principles—including acupuncture and herbal therapy—to address both symptoms and underlying patterns of imbalance.

How is TCM different from Western medicine?

While Western medicine often targets specific symptoms or diseases, TCM evaluates your overall health—including energy flow (Qi), organ function, lifestyle, and environment—to create a personalized treatment plan.

Is Traditional Chinese Medicine safe?

When provided by a licensed and experienced practitioner, TCM—including acupuncture and tailored herbal therapy—is considered a safe and well-tolerated approach to health care.

Is belief required for TCM to work?

No. TCM and acupuncture activate physiological responses and the body’s innate healing mechanisms, regardless of personal belief.

Acupuncture & herbs

What services does Shallowford Herbs & Acupuncture offer?

The clinic provides individualized acupuncture and herbal medicine designed to address chronic and acute concerns, support pain management, improve wellness, and re-establish balance throughout the body.

How does acupuncture help the body?

Acupuncture gently stimulates specific points on the body to improve circulation, regulate the nervous system, support immune function, and promote natural pain relief. It works by encouraging the body’s own healing response.

Does acupuncture hurt?

Acupuncture uses very thin, sterile needles and most patients experience minimal sensation. Many report feeling relaxed during and after treatment.

Do you use Chinese herbal medicine?

Yes. Herbal formulas are prescribed based on a thorough evaluation of your unique constitution and current condition. Herbs are blended to support the body’s balance and energy flow, often alongside acupuncture.

Your care

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit includes a detailed health consultation and assessment to understand your unique needs, followed by a treatment plan that may include acupuncture, herbs, or other supportive recommendations.

Is the treatment plan personalized?

Absolutely. Treatments are created for each patient based on a detailed assessment that may include diagnostic observation and discussion of your health history, symptom patterns, and wellness goals.

What can TCM help with?

Patients seek care for a wide range of concerns, including unresolved chronic conditions, pain, stress, digestive issues, sleep disturbances, and general wellness support—especially when conventional treatments have not fully relieved symptoms.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies depending on your condition, response to treatment, and overall health goals. Some patients notice improvement early, while others benefit from a structured course of care.

Do I need to change my lifestyle or diet?

Personalized lifestyle and dietary guidance may be offered to support your treatment and long-term health, but recommendations are practical and based on your individual habits and wellness goals.
